Buying a Travel Rule tool is not the same as running Travel Rule in production.

Failure mode:

- Tool shows green in demos.
- Hits and misses do not bind to the ticket.
- Dual control on the transfer does not see TR state.
- Evidence pack is a CSV export emailed at month-end.

## Production standard (plain language)

TR outcomes sit on the **same evidence plane** as the transfer decision.
Exceptions have a register.
Someone owns breaks.
