Tools can be live and the **source of truth** still be a spreadsheet.

Symptoms:

- Recon done in Excel after the chain moved.
- Approvals in email with no ticket PK.
- “Master” client list in a personal drive.
- Exception log that is really a Slack search.

## Production question

For the one workflow that matters: **where does the system of record live, and can dual control + evidence attach to it?**

If the answer is “several places,” you do not have production. You have a collage.

## What the sprint forces

An honest as-is map. Then a to-be where the evidence plane and control matrix point at systems you actually run — not a future platform fantasy.

We are not religious about vendors. We are religious about **one path you can defend**.
