Free diagnostics train the market to extract senior time and ghost.

Our public offers are **fixed-fee**. **50% on signature to start.** Remainder on delivery (or as written on the SOW).

## What the deposit buys both sides

- Calendar is real.
- Access and owners are real.
- Scope fights happen once, in writing.
- We do not run a charity discovery practice dressed as enterprise sales.

## What we will not do

- Multi-week unpaid “assessments” that recreate the sprint
- Starting work on verbal enthusiasm
- Expanding to a second workflow without a change order

Paid discovery (short, fixed) exists when a full sprint is premature — still paid.
