We used to sound like every other deck in the building: banks, SWIFT, CBDC curiosity, tokenization strategy, platform deploy next.

That story is wide. It is also **slow cash** for a small implementation firm without a VASP licence and without Big4 brand.

## Who we sell to now

Licensed operators with a **production gap** — especially mid-size VASPs where CCO/Ops can buy a fixed sprint.

## Who we deprioritize for Q4 cash

- Cold Tier-1 bank innovation labs
- Insurer/telecom “exploration”
- Unlicensed “help us launch a token” without counsel
- Mega-exchange HQ RFPs as first deals

Not because money never exists there — because **cycle time** and fence risk burn the only resource that matters before deposits: calendar in Dubai and Toronto.
