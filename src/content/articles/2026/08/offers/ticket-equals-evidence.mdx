Examiners do not want your mythology. They want a path from **decision → actor → artefact**.

If proof lives in:

- personal email,
- chat exports,
- desktop folders,
- or “we can rebuild it if asked,”

you do not have an evidence plane. You have archaeology.

## The production rule

**The ticket (or equivalent work object) is the primary key for evidence.**

Screenshots may attach. They do not replace the key.

Exports and reports should be reproducible from the same model — not handmade the night before a mock.

## What “evidence plane” means in a sprint

- Where proof lives today (honest as-is).
- Where it will live (to-be) on **your** systems.
- Retention and export shape.
- Linkage to dual control and exceptions.
- A pack structure a CCO can defend.

## Exam pressure

If a mock or exam is inside 60 days, do not start with a platform RFP. Start with an **Evidence War Room**: pack structure, gaps, dry-run checklist — still no pass promise, still no regulator liaison.
