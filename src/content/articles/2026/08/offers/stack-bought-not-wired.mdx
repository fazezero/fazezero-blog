Bull markets sell software. Production needs **wiring**.

Familiar failure mode:

- Custody platform live.
- Travel Rule tool live.
- KYC live.
- Tickets live.
- Dual control not enforced.
- TR hit not bound to evidence.
- Shared admin still smiling in the corner.

## What SI is

Fixed implementation work packages on **client-owned** configuration:

- Policy / dual-control settings you can defend
- TR path wired to ticket = evidence
- Shared-admin removal plan
- Export / rec job design
- Runbooks for handover

## What SI is not

- Rip-replace the vendor
- Host keys
- Compete as another custody product
- Open-ended “integration partner” without a fixed SOW

## How it enters the funnel

Best after a Production Sprint design, or via a **vendor PS/CS intro** on a stuck account. Cold “we’ll re-architect your stack” is usually the wrong open.
