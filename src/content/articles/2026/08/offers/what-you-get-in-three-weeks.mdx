If the output is only slides, you bought theatre.

The Production Sprint is built around a **folder** a decision-maker can act on.

## In the pack (typical)

1. Kick-off pack and RACI for one workflow
2. As-is map — people, systems, tickets, evidence gaps
3. To-be operating model — dual control, gates, exceptions
4. Requirements and use cases (when the thick pack applies)
5. Architecture views — context, sequences, components, data/control flow
6. Control matrix mapped to that workflow
7. Evidence-plane design on your systems
8. 90-day production path, SOP outlines, go-live checklist
9. Executive one-pager for CCO or board

Exact composition is in the [Production Sprint brief](https://fazezero.com/offers/production-sprint). Samples of shape (not a paid client case) live under [Work](https://fazezero.com/work).

## What “done” means

- Named workflow has a clear to-be.
- CCO can point to control + evidence model.
- 90-day path is accepted or explicitly deferred with owners.

Not: “we aligned stakeholders.” Not: “platform roadmap.”

## After the sprint

Optional: Production Build, Integration SI, Operator Lab, fractional Production Owner.
None of those are forced. None replace the deposit-backed sprint.
