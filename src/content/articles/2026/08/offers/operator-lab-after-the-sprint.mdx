Hiring is slow. Volume is not.

Operator Lab is **not** a public “crypto course.” It is practice on the operating model — dual control, evidence, exceptions — with a take-home CLIENT folder, not slideware only.

## Rule we enforce

**After the sprint is paid** (or equivalent design exists).
Otherwise you train people on fog.

## Formats

- Closed firm workshop (1–2 days), or
- Seats when a cohort makes sense

Deposit holds the date.

## What success looks like

Operators can run the path without the consultant in the chair.
CCO still owns accountability. We do not take keys or become the shadow ops team through training alone.

[Operator Lab](https://fazezero.com/offers/operator-lab)

**Next step:** If the sprint folder exists and the team cannot execute it, Lab is the buy — not another strategy offsite.

*Fence: Training on production ops/evidence. Not licensing education. Not advice on buying or selling assets.*
