Some problems are design problems. Some are **calendar** problems.

If mock or exam is inside roughly 60 days, a three-week architecture romance may be the wrong buy. You need a pack path, gap burn-down, and a dry-run — fast — without pretending a consultant can guarantee a pass.

## What the War Room is

**1–2 weeks. Fixed fee.**
Examiner-oriented structure for **one** workflow:

- Where evidence lives today
- Pack layout by question themes you actually face
- Critical gaps with owners and dates
- Exception register design
- Dry-run checklist

## What it is not

- A shorter, discounted Production Sprint
- A pass promise
- Counsel or regulator representation
- A rewrite of your entire policy suite

## When to buy the Sprint instead

If you have time and the book is wrong at the root (no dual control model, no to-be, no 90-day path), buy **SKU-01**. Use the War Room when the date is the constraint.

## Commercial reality

Deposit to start. Same week if you can staff access. If you cannot name an owner, do not buy.
