“Compliance advisory” is a phrase that hides three different jobs:

1. **Legal and licensing** — counsel.
2. **Policy theatre** — documents nobody runs.
3. **Production controls and evidence** — what operators actually need on Tuesday.

We only sell (3), productized.

## What we will write

- Control matrices tied to a workflow
- Evidence-plane design
- Dual-control operating models
- Exam-oriented packs (no pass promise)

## What we will not sell as a product

- Jurisdiction shopping
- “We’ll get you licensed”
- Securities or virtual-asset opinions
- Speaking to the regulator as your representative
- Generic AML opinions

If your RFP is mostly (1), hire counsel.
If your pain is (3), see [Offers](https://fazezero.com/offers).

## Why this is commercial, not only ethical

Blurred advisory is how boutiques get two empty years of “strategic conversations.” Fixed production SOWs with deposits are how cash shows up.
